Our Story

Serving Central Texas Since 1998

Verdict started as a two-attorney office focused on personal injury cases. Over the years we added family law, business law, real estate, criminal defense and estate planning, always with the same approach: prepare every case as if it will go to trial.

Today the firm has nine attorneys and a support team of paralegals and legal assistants. Most of our new clients come to us through referrals from former clients and other lawyers.
