What to Do After a Car Accident in Texas

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Blog Details
March 3, 2025 Michael Carter Personal Injury

The days right after a collision are stressful, and a few simple steps can protect both your health and your claim. Here is the checklist we give to new clients.

1. Get Medical Attention

See a doctor the same day even if you feel fine. Some injuries show up later, and a gap between the accident and your first visit is something insurance companies will use against you.

2. Document Everything

Take photos of the vehicles, the scene and your injuries. Get the other driver's insurance details and the names of any witnesses. Request a copy of the police report.

Do not give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company before speaking with an attorney.

3. Keep Your Records

Save medical bills, repair estimates and proof of missed work. These documents are the basis of your claim.

4. Talk to an Attorney
