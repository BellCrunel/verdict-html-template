Michael Carter

Founding Partner, Personal Injury

Michael founded Verdict in 1998 after five years at a national defense firm, where he learned how insurance companies evaluate and fight injury claims. He has represented injured Texans ever since, with a focus on trucking accidents and serious injury cases.

He is board-certified in personal injury trial law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization and has been named a Texas Super Lawyer for the past eight years. Michael also lectures on trial practice and mentors every associate in the firm through their first trials.
