Personal Injury Attorneys

If you were injured because of someone else's negligence, you may be entitled to compensation for medical bills, lost income and pain and suffering. Our attorneys have handled injury claims across Central Texas for more than two decades and know how insurance companies evaluate a case.

We take personal injury cases on a contingency fee. That means you pay nothing up front and no fee at all unless we recover money for you.

Cases We Handle

  • Car, truck and motorcycle accidents
  • Slip and fall and premises liability
  • Workplace injuries and workers' compensation
  • Medical negligence
  • Wrongful death claims

How We Work

After a free consultation we gather the police report, medical records and witness statements, deal with the insurance adjusters on your behalf and prepare the case for trial. Most claims settle, but insurers offer fair settlements only when they know the firm is ready to go to court.

In Texas the general deadline is two years from the date of the injury. Some claims have shorter deadlines, so contact us as early as possible.

It depends on your medical costs, lost wages and the strength of the evidence. We will give you an honest estimate after reviewing your case.

Most cases settle out of court. If a fair settlement is not offered, we are prepared to take your case to trial.
